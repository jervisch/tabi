# TABi — Camera / Film Treatment Prompt

Design a custom camera experience for 旅 TABi.

## Visual identity

The camera should feel like a Japanese travel-journal camera, not a generic iOS camera.

Use:
- washi/cream UI surfaces where visible
- sumi text
- vermilion shutter accent
- Japanese + English location labels
- minimal controls
- soft photographic preview
- subtle paper/editorial details

## TABi photographic treatment

Create a restrained, consistent look:
- warm highlights
- muted saturation
- soft contrast
- lifted blacks
- gentle highlight rolloff
- subtle grain
- subtle halation
- natural skin tones
- preserve reds and greens

Avoid:
- heavy vintage effects
- fake light leaks
- huge borders
- date stamps burned into every photo
- excessive grain

The effect should make a collection of photos feel like one coherent Japanese travel journal.
